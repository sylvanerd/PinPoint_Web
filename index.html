Privacy & Consent Notice

Before the customer starts browsing with the smart glasses, walk through this notice together. It explains what PinPoint records, how it is used, and what choices they have.

Filling consent for: Sylvan Marchetti

Before you start, please read this short notice. It explains what PinPoint records while you browse with the smart glasses, how that information is used, and what choices you have. It takes about a minute.

What we record
  • Voice notes you speak while browsing (e.g. "I love this handle style but the color is too cold"), automatically transcribed to text.
  • Photos of the products and reference images (e.g. a magazine page) you choose to capture or crop.
  • The location of each note relative to the product it was attached to, and the order in which you visited products.
  • An AI-generated summary of your preferences (style, colour, material, budget signals) built from the above.
How it's used
  • To help our sales team understand what you're interested in before they approach you, so the conversation starts from where you left off instead of from scratch.
  • To let a salesperson show you a recommended product, true to its real size, directly in your view.
  • In aggregate and de-identified form, to understand how customers move through and respond to the showroom.
Your choices
  • Participation is optional. If you'd rather browse without recording, tell any member of staff and the glasses will run in view-only mode — no voice, photo, or note capture.
  • You can review, edit, or delete your notes and profile at any time by asking a member of staff.
  • If you leave your email below, we'll send you a short recap of what you saved during this visit. Leaving it blank has no effect on your ability to use the glasses.
  • Your profile is kept so it can be recognised if you return, and is automatically deleted after 6 months of inactivity, or sooner on request.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising outside this showroom.
